Audited statement

The review of candidate 0.1.0 found no transmission of gameplay data to Dharma Labs or third parties. Progress and preferences stay in the app's private storage. If Android Auto Backup is enabled, the system may back up that file under the user's Google account without giving Dharma Labs access.

Platform Portrait Android; min SDK 24, target and compile SDK 36
Version 0.1.0, versionCode 1
Permissions Normal system vibration; no INTERNET or dangerous runtime permissions
Embedded SDKs Phaser 3.90.0 and Capacitor 8 for app lifecycle, preferences, and haptics; no Google Play Services, ads, or telemetry
Connectivity Gameplay needs no Internet. The visible legal link opens the system browser only when the user taps it
Accounts and interaction No accounts, chat, user content, or multiplayer
Backup Android may back up only RunningMeili.xml
Target audience Play Console age-group selection has not been verified; the build has no age profile or age gate
Contact contacto@dharma-uy.com
Audit scope: code, dependencies, Android configuration, and the candidate artifact. The exact legal identity shown by the Play Console account, distribution countries, target age groups, and final signed AAB must be confirmed before review.
